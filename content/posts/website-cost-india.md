Everyone wants a number, and nobody wants to be the first to say it. So here's an honest look at what a website actually costs in India, and what changes that number.

## What actually changes the price

A website isn't one thing. A simple one-page site and a full online store are both "websites," but the work behind them is completely different. The main things that move the cost:

- **How many pages** you need, and how custom each one is
- **Whether it's just information** or people need to log in, book, or pay
- **Who writes the content** — you, or the team building it
- **How custom the design is** versus a ready template

## The honest ranges

We don't put fixed prices on our site because every project is genuinely different. But you're not wrong to want a ballpark before a call.

A clean, custom informational website for a small business is very different from an e-commerce platform with payments and inventory. The gap between the two is large, and any agency giving you one flat number without asking questions is guessing.

## How to avoid overpaying

The biggest waste isn't the build — it's paying for things you don't need yet. Start with what earns you customers now, and add the rest when there's a reason to.
